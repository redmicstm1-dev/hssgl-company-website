# Local website assets

All website photography, icon SVGs, and the favicon are stored in `public/`. The site does not load image files or icons from external hosts or CDNs.

## Hero and supporting photography
- `images/hero-security.webp` and `images/hero-security-guard.webp` — wide, dark-navy hero visual used on the homepage and inner-page headers.
- `images/security-guard.webp`, `images/security-team.webp`, `images/security-camera.webp`, `images/monitoring-room.webp`, `images/professional-security.webp`, `images/commercial-building.webp`, `images/hotel-hospitality.webp`, `images/industrial-facility.webp`, `images/residential-estate.webp`, and `images/business-partnership.webp` — existing supporting photos from the asset set.
- `images/service-*.webp` — six photo-led service cards matching the earlier service wireframe.
- `images/industry-*.webp` — nine industry card images matching the earlier industries wireframe.
- `images/contact-office.webp` and `images/request-map.webp` — contact and request-page supporting visuals. The map is illustrative, not a verified business location.

## Local SVG icons
All icons are custom local SVG files in `public/icons/`, including service, industry, contact, navigation, upload, process, and trust icons. The icon set uses a consistent navy stroke style with the project’s navy/gold asset palette.

## Important brand note
The brand board used for visual matching contained a sample brand name. That sample logo is deliberately not installed as the client’s logo. Keep the neutral shield favicon until the client supplies the real logo and confirms brand colours.

## Image provenance
The service and industry image files are locally stored crops from the project’s generated visual asset board, sized for website card use. The hero background is the original separate wide image. Replace illustrative imagery with client-approved operational photography if supplied.
